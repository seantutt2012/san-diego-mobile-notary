MOBILE NOTARY QUESTIONS

How do I arrange a mobile notary appointment?

Call or text Rebecca at 619-571-0088 with your location, the type of document, and your preferred appointment time. Contact her to confirm availability and meeting details.

Where can Rebecca meet me?

Rebecca provides mobile notary appointments throughout San Diego County, including homes, offices, hospitals, assisted living facilities, and other convenient meeting locations. Contact her to confirm your location and any facility access requirements.

What should I have ready for my appointment?

When booking, tell Rebecca which documents need notarization and how many people will be signing. Ask her to confirm the identification, witnesses, and other preparation needed for your particular appointment.

What types of documents can I ask about?

Rebecca offers general notarizations, acknowledgments, jurats, affidavits, and oaths and affirmations. You can also ask about notarization of powers of attorney, deeds, trusts, and health care directives. Describe your documents when booking so she can confirm the service you need.
