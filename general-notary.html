GENERAL NOTARY APPOINTMENTS

When getting to a notary's office is inconvenient, a mobile appointment brings the service to you. Rebecca serves customers throughout San Diego County. Contact her directly to discuss your documents, confirm your meeting location, and arrange an appointment.

Services you can ask about

  • General document notarization
  • Acknowledgments and jurats
  • Affidavits
  • Oaths and affirmations

Have a power of attorney, deed, trust, or health care directive? Describe the document when you call so Rebecca can confirm the service needed for your appointment.

Appointments at your location

Mobile visits are available at homes and offices, as well as hospitals and assisted living facilities. If you are arranging a visit for someone else, coordinate the preferred location and any facility access requirements with Rebecca when booking.

Service areas include San Diego, Coronado, Chula Vista, La Jolla, El Cajon, Poway, Rancho Bernardo, Rancho Santa Fe, Del Mar, Carlsbad, and Oceanside. Ask Rebecca to confirm your specific location.

HOW TO BOOK YOUR VISIT

  1. Describe your appointment. Call or text with your location, document type, number of signers, and preferred time.
  2. Confirm the details. Rebecca can confirm availability, the meeting location, and the quote for your visit.
  3. Prepare for the meeting. Ask Rebecca what identification, witnesses, and other preparation your particular appointment requires.

If your paperwork includes instructions from the organization requesting it, mention those when arranging the appointment.
